DR.

I believe music is so much more than how we typically look at it. It not only creates a moment for our busy minds to completely dissolve from the world of thinking into the world of sensing. But it transforms us.

Bringing us back to when we were young. Our little hand pointing towards that moving thing in the sky. The colors, shapes and lights touching us deeply. Taking us even further back to how we lived before the modern world. Families around the fire making songs to connect with the divine.

Music resonates like nature resonates. Sun is sun. Rain is rain. There's nothing to think or analyze. It's boundless yet simple and clear.

Most of all...

music is coming home
