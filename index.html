Music is everywhere

I believe music is so much more than we often realize. It allows our busy minds to dissolve, even for a moment, taking us away from a world of thinking and into a world of feeling. And in doing so, it transforms us.

It brings us back to when we were young. To the moment when we pointed our little hands towards something moving in the sky, captivated by the colors, shapes, and lights that touched us so deeply. It takes us even further back, to a time before the modern world, when families gathered around a fire, making music together to connect with the divine.

So I think music resonates like nature resonates. Sun is sun. Rain is rain. There's nothing to think about or analyze. It's boundless, yet simple and clear.

Most of all, music brings us back to where we belong.

Music is coming home
